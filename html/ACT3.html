Sinigang na Baboy

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg pork belly
  • 1 pack tamarind soup mix
  • 1 bunch kangkong
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 eggplant
  • String beans and gabi

Steps:

  1. Boil pork with onion and tomatoes.
  2. Add tamarind mix and simmer until tender.
  3. Put in vegetables and season with fish sauce and pepper.

Chicken Adobo

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tablespoon peppercorns

Steps:

  1. Marinate chicken in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and peppercorns.
  2. Simmer until cooked and sauce thickens.
  3. Serve hot with steamed rice.

Lechon Kawali

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg pork belly
  • Salt and pepper
  • Water for boiling
  • Cooking oil for frying

Steps:

  1. Boil pork belly until tender.
  2. Cool, season, and deep fry until crispy.
  3. Slice and serve with garlic vinegar dip.
