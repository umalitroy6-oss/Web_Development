LIFE & CAREER SKILLS

  1. Lesson 1: Arnold Gesell's Maturation Theory

    • Summary: Gesell's theory explains that human growth follows a natural and orderly pattern over time, showing that development happens gradually and is influenced by both biology and experience.
    • Reflection: I learned that growth cannot be rushed and that every person develops at their own pace, which taught me to be more patient with myself and with others.
    • Application: I reminded myself that learning new skills takes time and practice, so I became more understanding of my progress and the importance of consistent effort.

  2. Lesson 2: Erik Erikson's Psychosocial Theory

    • Summary: Erikson's theory explains that people grow through social and emotional challenges at different stages of life, and these experiences shape identity, confidence, and relationships.
    • Reflection: I realized that personal development is a long process and that identities are formed gradually through experiences, responsibilities, and meaningful interactions with other people.
    • Application: I tried new activities and joined different groups so I could explore my interests, discover my strengths, and become more confident in my own growth.

  3. Lesson 3: Daniel Goleman's Emotional Intelligence Theory

    • Summary: Emotional intelligence involves understanding emotions, showing empathy, managing relationships well, and making positive choices that support both personal growth and social harmony.
    • Reflection: I learned that emotional skills are just as important as academic ability because they affect communication, teamwork, patience, and how we respond to others.
    • Application: I started paying closer attention to how other people feel before sharing my own thoughts, which helped me respond with more kindness and better judgment.

GENERAL MATHEMATICS

  1. Lesson 1: Understanding and Computing Salary, Wage and Commission

    • Summary: Salary, wage, and commission are different forms of income, and each one is calculated in a unique way depending on the structure of pay and the kind of work involved.
    • Reflection: I became more aware that understanding these terms is important because income calculations affect personal finances, budgeting, and decisions about work and career growth.
    • Application: I practiced solving sample problems involving pay rates and earnings so I could strengthen my mathematical skills and better understand real-world financial situations.

  2. Lesson 2: Computing Holiday and Non-Holiday

    • Summary: Holiday and non-holiday pay computations are important in workplace math because different days may require different rates, and these rules affect how employees are paid.
    • Reflection: I learned that basic mathematics helps people understand labor rules, compensation systems, and the importance of fairness in financial transactions and work arrangements.
    • Application: I solved practice problems about regular days, rest days, and holidays so I could improve my accuracy and better appreciate the practical use of mathematics at work.

EFFECTIVE COMMUNICATION

  1. Lesson 1: Intrapersonal and Interpersonal Communication

    • Summary: Intrapersonal communication happens inside a person, while interpersonal communication happens between people, and both are essential in shaping how we think, speak, and relate to others.
    • Reflection: I noticed that understanding myself first helps me speak more clearly and respond more thoughtfully because communication begins with awareness and self-control.
    • Application: I practiced organizing my thoughts before speaking and listening carefully during conversations so I could improve the quality of my communication in everyday situations.

  2. Lesson 2: Controlled and Uncontrolled Interpersonal Communication

    • Summary: Controlled communication is planned and careful, while uncontrolled communication is often spontaneous and emotional, and both can strongly affect how messages are received and understood.
    • Reflection: I learned that reacting too quickly can cause misunderstandings, while pausing to think can lead to better conversations, stronger relationships, and wiser decisions.
    • Application: I tried to slow down before responding in stressful situations so I could choose my words carefully and communicate with greater respect and clarity.

  3. Lesson 3: Language and Tone

    • Summary: Language carries the message, while tone influences how that message is felt, interpreted, and received by other people, especially in personal and professional communication.
    • Reflection: I understood that even a correct message can create conflict if the tone is harsh, careless, or disrespectful, so emotional delivery matters as much as content.
    • Application: I made an effort to use a calmer and friendlier tone when giving advice or feedback so that my words would be more helpful and less likely to hurt others.

GENERAL SCIENCE

  1. Lesson 1:Law Of Inertia

    • Summary: Physics is the study of matter, motion, force, and energy, and it helps explain why everyday objects move, fall, or stay still in the way they do around us.
    • Reflection: I learned that physics is not only found in laboratories but also in ordinary life because many daily experiences are connected to scientific principles and natural laws.
    • Application: I used simple physics concepts to understand how force and motion affect the behavior of objects, especially when they are moved, pushed, or weighed in everyday situations.

  2. Lesson 2: Law Of Acceleration

    • Summary: Translational motion involves movement from one place to another, while rotational motion involves turning around an axis, and both are important in understanding how objects move.
    • Reflection: I understood that many moving objects do not follow only one type of motion because they can show both translation and rotation at the same time in different ways.
    • Application: I observed how rolling objects demonstrate these motions in real life, which helped me connect theory with actual movement and improve my understanding of motion.

  3. Lesson 3: Law Of Action-Reaction

    • Summary: Newton's laws explain how force affects motion, how objects resist changes in movement, and why different actions produce different results depending on mass and acceleration.
    • Reflection: I realized that these laws are very relevant in daily life because they help explain common situations such as walking, driving, falling, and stopping safely.
    • Application: I applied the idea of inertia and force to everyday examples so I could better understand why safety measures, braking systems, and careful movement are important.

CPNT (.NET)

  1. Lesson 1: Introduction To HTML

    • Summary: HTML gives structure to web content by using tags that help browsers understand how to organize text, images, links, and other media in a clear and meaningful way for users.
    • Reflection: I realized that even the smallest HTML tags can make a webpage easier to read and more professional because they create a strong foundation for everything that follows.
    • Application: I practiced creating headings, paragraphs, and lists in a simple webpage so I could apply the basic structure of HTML and observe how content becomes easier to organize.

  2. Lesson 2: Integrated Development Environment (IDE)

    • Summary: An IDE is a useful software tool that combines coding, editing, debugging, and testing features in one place, allowing developers to work more efficiently and stay organized.
    • Reflection: I learned that using an IDE makes programming less stressful because it reduces confusion, helps catch mistakes faster, and gives better control over the whole workflow.
    • Application: I used an editor and browser together to test my work, organize my files properly, and understand how a proper development environment supports better output.

  3. Lesson 3: HTML Attributes

    • Summary: HTML attributes provide additional information to elements, allowing developers to improve usability, control behavior, and make web pages more interactive and functional.
    • Reflection: I understood that tags alone are not enough because attributes add important details that help browsers interpret and display content in more meaningful ways.
    • Application: I practiced using attributes to improve links and images so I could see how small additions can make a webpage clearer, more informative, and more user-friendly.

  4. Lesson 4: Apply List Attributes And Value In Webpage Designs

    • Summary: List attributes help control the appearance and structure of lists, giving designers more freedom to organize information neatly and improve the visual order of content on a page.
    • Reflection: I noticed that choosing the right list style does more than change appearance because it also affects readability, flow, and the overall quality of a website layout.
    • Application: I experimented with different list styles and numbering formats so I could understand how list design can make information easier to follow and more visually appealing.

PAG-AARAL NG KASAYSAYAN AT LIPUNANG PILIPINO

  1. Lesson 1: Kahulugan ng Kasaysayan at Kamalayang Kasaysayan

    • Summary: History is the study of the past, while historical awareness is the understanding of its importance in the present, in shaping national identity, and in developing a country's perspective.
    • Reflection: I learned that history is not just about events that happened long ago but also about the lessons and experiences that continue to shape us today.
    • Application: I shared lessons from history with my peers to expand their understanding and teach them the importance of remembering and respecting our past.

  2. Lesson 2: Ugnayan ng Heograpiya at Kultura

    • Summary: Geography affects culture, lifestyle, and traditions of a place because of climate, landforms, and natural resources that surround the people living there.
    • Reflection: I realized that our culture comes not only from beliefs but also from our environment, lifestyle, and experiences that shape our identity and values.
    • Application: I compared different places and ways of living to better understand how geography influences culture and the traditions of people worldwide.

EMILIAN CULTURE

  1. Lesson 1: Parable of the Sins

    • Summary: The parable teaches that people must recognize their mistakes, accept responsibility for their actions, and learn from their wrong choices in order to grow in character and wisdom.
    • Reflection: I realized that admitting mistakes is a sign of maturity and honesty because it shows courage, humility, and a willingness to improve rather than hide from the truth.
    • Application: I started correcting my errors immediately and trying to learn from them instead of avoiding them, which helped me become more responsible and self-aware.

  2. Lesson 2: Five Pillars of Learning

    • Summary: The five pillars of learning highlight that education is not only about knowledge but also about skills, values, attitudes, and personal growth that continue beyond the classroom.
    • Reflection: I learned that learning should not stop after school because everyday experiences also teach us important lessons about discipline, responsibility, service, and self-improvement.
    • Application: I tried to apply what I learned in class to real situations at home and in school so that my knowledge would become meaningful and useful in daily life.

MABISANG KOMUNIKASYON

  1. Lesson 1: Wika

    • Summary: Ang wika ay isang mahalagang kasangkapan sa komunikasyon dahil pinapayagan nito ang tao na maipahayag ang kanilang iniisip, damdamin, at kultura sa paraang malinaw, maayos, at may kahulugan.
    • Reflection: Napagtanto ko na ang wika ay hindi lamang simpleng salita kundi isang mahalagang bahagi ng pagkakakilanlan at pag-unawa sa sarili at sa kapwa.
    • Application: Sinubukan kong gumamit ng mas maingat at magalang na pananalita sa pakikipag-usap upang mapanatili ang pag-unawa, respeto, at magandang relasyon sa iba.

  2. Lesson 2: Intrapersonal at Interpersonal na Komunikasyon

    • Summary: Ang intrapersonal na komunikasyon ay nangyayari sa loob ng isip, samantalang ang interpersonal na komunikasyon ay nagaganap sa pagitan ng dalawang tao, at pareho silang mahalaga sa pagbuo ng pag-unawa.
    • Reflection: Natutunan ko na ang pagninilay bago magsalita ay nakakatulong upang maging mas malinaw ang aking mga ideya at mas maayos ang pakikipag-ugnayan sa iba.
    • Application: Sinusubukan kong pag-isipin muna ang aking sasabihin bago ako makisali sa isang usapan, lalo na kapag mahalaga ang mga bagay na dapat kong ipahayag.

  3. Lesson 3: Kontrolado at Di-Kontroladong Intrapersonal at Interpersonal

    • Summary: Ang kontroladong komunikasyon ay mahinahon at may pag-iisip, samantalang ang di-kontroladong komunikasyon ay madalas na reaksyon sa damdamin, at ang dalawa ay may malaking epekto sa paraan ng pag-uusap.
    • Reflection: Naunawaan ko na ang pag-iwas sa impulsibong pagsagot ay mahalaga dahil nakakatulong ito upang maiwasan ang hindi magandang saloobin at mas mapabuti ang relasyon sa iba.
    • Application: Nagsasanay ako na huminto at mag-isip muna bago sumagot kapag nagagalit ako, upang mapanatili ko ang katahimikan at maipahayag ang aking saloobin nang mahinahon.

  4. Lesson 4: Linggwahe at Tono

    • Summary: Ang linggwahe ang nagdadala ng mensahe, habang ang tono ang nagtatakda kung paano ito tatanggapin at maiintindihan ng nakikinig, kaya mahalaga ang pareho sa komunikasyon.
    • Reflection: Napagtanto ko na kahit tama ang sinasabi mo, maaari pa rin itong makaapekto nang masama kung hindi mo isinaalang-alang ang tono ng iyong pananalita.
    • Application: Sinisikap kong gumamit ng mahinahon at mapagkalingang tono kapag nagbibigay ako ng puna, payo, o kahit simpleng paliwanag sa ibang tao.
